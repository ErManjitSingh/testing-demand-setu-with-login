Day-wise Itinerary

Days 1-3
1
Shimla
Arrival in Delhi & Transfer to Shimla

Arrive in Delhi—India's bustling capital—and begin your scenic transfer to Shimla, the charming hill town once known as the "Summer Capital of British India."

As you leave behind the urban landscape, the road gradually winds upward into Himachal Pradesh. Pass through towns like Karnal, Chandigarh, and Solan, with changing views from flat farmlands to misty pine forests and hillside hamlets.

Optionally, stop for lunch at a roadside dhaba for a taste of North Indian flavors, or refresh at a viewpoint as the hills draw closer.

Reach Shimla by evening. Known for its colonial architecture, Mall Road charm, and views of the snow-dusted Shivalik ranges, Shimla offers the perfect welcome to the mountains.

Take a short evening stroll on The Ridge or enjoy a hot cup of tea with valley views as the hill town lights up under the twilight.

Overnight stay in Shimla, breathing in the crisp mountain air and preparing for the adventures ahead.

2
Sangla
Shimla to Sangla via Narkanda

Depart Shimla after breakfast and drive through the scenic mountain roads towards Sangla Valley. En route, pass through Narkanda — a charming hill station known for apple orchards and panoramic Himalayan views.

As you descend into the Baspa Valley, witness the landscape transform into lush green meadows, pine forests, and the gushing Baspa River. Arrive in Sangla by evening and check into your cozy cottage.

Overnight stay in Sangla.

3
Chitkul
Sangla to Chitkul

After breakfast, embark on a scenic drive to Chitkul — the last inhabited village near the Indo-Tibet border. Known as the 'Last Village of India', Chitkul offers breathtaking views of snow-capped peaks and the pristine Baspa River.

Explore the quaint village, visit the ancient Mathi Temple, and soak in the untouched beauty of this remote Himalayan hamlet.

Overnight stay in Chitkul.

Himalaya EscapesPage 7

Day-wise Itinerary

Days 4-6
4
Kalpa
Chitkul to Kalpa

Drive from Chitkul to Kalpa, one of the most picturesque villages in Kinnaur district. Kalpa is famous for its stunning views of the Kinner Kailash mountain range and traditional Kinnauri architecture.

Visit the Suicide Point for dramatic cliff-edge views, explore the ancient Narayan-Nagini Temple, and stroll through apple orchards.

Overnight stay in Kalpa.

5
Tabo
Kalpa to Tabo

Begin your journey into the mystical Spiti Valley. Drive through the dramatic landscapes of Kinnaur, crossing high-altitude passes with breathtaking views of barren mountains and deep gorges.

Arrive in Tabo — home to the ancient Tabo Monastery, over 1000 years old and often called the 'Ajanta of the Himalayas'. Explore the monastery's exquisite murals and meditation caves.

Overnight stay in Tabo.

6
Kaza
Tabo to Kaza

Continue your Spiti adventure with a drive to Kaza — the capital of Spiti Valley. En route, visit Dhankar Monastery perched dramatically on a cliff, and the fossil-rich village of Langza.

Arrive in Kaza by evening. This bustling town serves as the perfect base for exploring the wonders of Spiti Valley.

Overnight stay in Kaza.

Himalaya EscapesPage 8

Day-wise Itinerary

Days 7-9
7
Kaza
Kaza Complete Local Sightseeing

Full day of local sightseeing around Kaza. Visit the iconic Key Monastery — one of the largest and oldest Buddhist monasteries in Spiti, perched at 4,166 meters.

Explore Kibber Village — one of the highest inhabited villages in the world, visit the picturesque Hikkim village (home to the world's highest post office), and the beautiful Komik village.

Return to Kaza for overnight stay.

8
Manali
Kaza to Manali via Chandratal

Embark on one of the most adventurous drives — crossing the Kunzum Pass and optionally visiting the stunning Chandratal Lake (Moon Lake) with its crystal-clear blue waters surrounded by snow peaks.

Continue through the dramatic Spiti-Lahaul landscape to Manali, crossing the famous Rohtang Pass.

Overnight stay in Manali.

9
Delhi
Departure from Manali to Delhi

After breakfast, check out from your hotel and begin your return journey to Delhi. Drive through the scenic Kullu Valley with views of the Beas River and pine-covered hills.

Arrive in Delhi by evening with unforgettable memories of your Spiti Valley adventure. Tour concludes with cherished Himalayan experiences.
